ATTRIBUTION

Contains information from GeoNames (https://www.geonames.org/), licensed
under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).

The database packages in this release (CN.sqlite.gz, JP.sqlite.gz,
US.sqlite.gz, countries.sqlite.gz) are derived works of GeoNames
gazetteer data. The data has been filtered, normalized, localized
(simplified Chinese conversion via OpenCC) and converted to SQLite for
offline administrative-area selection.
